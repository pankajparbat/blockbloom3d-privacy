# Block Bloom 3D privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers the Block Bloom 3D game for Android, published by [developer name as shown on Google Play]. It explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- There are no accounts. Your scores, settings and progress are saved only on your phone.
- Ads are shown by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show ads and measure them.
- Gameplay statistics go to Unity Analytics to help improve the game. They contain no name or contact details.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked before ads are personalised, and gameplay statistics are not collected at all.

## Advertising: Google AdMob

The game shows a banner along the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad between some games, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. These ads come from Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
- your approximate location, worked out from your IP address
- information about your device, such as its model and operating system
- how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google's use of this information is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices.** In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears the first time you play. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen by tapping Privacy. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).

## Gameplay statistics: Unity Analytics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics, run by Unity Technologies. Examples: a level started or finished, a game over with its score, a power-up used, an ad watched, a purchase made.

Unity Analytics also records an identifier for this installation of the game, your device model and operating system, your country (worked out from your IP address) and when you play. It does not receive your name, email address, contacts or exact location.

Gameplay statistics are **not** collected in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, or anywhere else Google's consent check says consent is required. Unity's handling of this information is covered by the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and Bloom Storms). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: the game never sees your card or payment details. It receives only an order number, which it keeps on your phone so a purchase is never applied twice.

## What stays on your phone

Your scores, settings, coins, game progress and purchase order numbers are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Reinstalling the game also gives it a new Unity Analytics identifier.

## Sharing your results

If you tap Share, your phone's own share menu opens with your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes.

## Children

Block Bloom 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want information about them deleted, contact us at the address below.
